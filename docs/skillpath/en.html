Privacy Policy

Updated November 16, 2026

SkillPath helps you organize goals into learning paths and track your practice. This policy explains how the SkillPath team handles data when you use the Android and iOS app.

Delete my accountContact the team

Guests, accounts and profiles

As a guest, you can try sample activities without entering an email address or password. When you create an account, your email and password go to Supabase for registration, email confirmation, sign-in and account recovery. An account identifier connects your profile and records. You choose which profile details to enter, such as your name, bio, avatar and interests. Profile information saved to your account is handled separately from path synchronization.

On your device and in the cloud

The app stores your paths, activities, schedule, review answers, progress, notes and measurements on your device. What you record depends on your goal and can include personal information such as a spiritual reflection or a financial target. Practice reminders are optional and scheduled on your device.

If you enable sync, the app sends your workspace records to Supabase under your account. Photos, audio and documents that you select are uploaded to private storage only when you request a file upload. Access to cloud files requires authentication and is restricted to the corresponding account.

Service providers and security

Supabase provides authentication, database and file storage services. Connections to the service use HTTPS. On mobile, the app stores your sign-in session in the operating system’s protected credential storage. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical data such as IP addresses, request times and errors to deliver the service and investigate problems.

When the SkillPath Plus integration is active, Google Play or the App Store processes purchases. RevenueCat uses your account identifier and subscription details such as product, expiration and renewal status to verify access to paid features. You provide payment details to the store.

Backups and sharing

A .skillpath export can include records and attachments available on your device. A JSON export contains records and attachment references. Exported backups are unencrypted. You decide where to store them and who can open them.

Delete your account and data

In the app. Open Settings, choose Account and security, find the option to delete your account and data, and confirm with your password.

Without the app. From your registered email address, request account deletion by email. Say that you want your SkillPath account deleted. The team may verify ownership. Never send your password.

Your profile and synced workspace are kept under your account while it is active. Deletion removes your account, profile, synced workspace and uploaded attachments from the service. A technical receipt remains without a link to your account. Providers’ security logs and operational backups follow their retention cycles. Remove exported copies, shared files and local records on other devices wherever you stored them. If you subscribe to Plus, manage cancellation in the store where you purchased it.

Privacy contact

To access, correct or request deletion of your data, email the SkillPath team at contato.tavern@gmail.com. The team uses your email and the details needed to verify and respond to your request.
